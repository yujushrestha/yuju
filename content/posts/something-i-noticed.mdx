i've been thinking about the way light changes just before it disappears.

not sunset exactly. the moment after. when the sky doesn't know what color it is yet.

there's a word in japanese — *ma* — which means the pause between notes. the silence that gives music its shape.

i think about that a lot when i'm writing code. the space between functions. the breath before a return statement.

maybe that's what i'm always looking for. not the thing itself. the space around it.
